# Introduction completeness and teaching review

This review asks a different question from source fidelity: does each selected introduction give its intended learner enough background to enter the subject's central discussions?

## Scope and standard

Read all 98 current entries and the six handbook framing statements. Preserve the existing scope: selected introductions in six areas, not exhaustive coverage of Shas or a practical law manual. A consciously excluded topic is not automatically a defect. A term promised by an included title still needs an intelligible explanation.

For each entry, assess whether the learner can identify the subject, understand its central acts or relationships, distinguish the main cases, and follow the relevant adjacent entries. Identify missing prerequisites, unexplained vocabulary that carries the explanation, incomplete comparisons, and repetition that occupies space without advancing understanding. Do not substitute word counts or equal-length templates for these judgments.

## Rambam and other approaches

Keep Rambam as the main organizing framework. Assess the actual sources: the corpus also uses Mishnah, Scripture, Meiri, Rashi, Ramban and other commentators. Citation diversity alone does not establish balanced coverage.

Add a major alternative only when omitting it would materially mislead the intended learner or obstruct the central discussion being introduced. Distinguish an explicitly attributed account of Rambam from an assertion that all authorities agree. Do not demand a survey of every disagreement or manufacture alternatives to make the book appear broader.

## Evidence and proposed work

Record an entry-specific judgment, learner question, relevant prerequisites, exact blocks inspected, and a concrete next action where needed. Separate core explanatory gaps, optional enrichment, and candidates that still need source verification. New substantive wording requires identified source support, relevant context and an accessible textual witness where possible. Record edition/access limits; a public transcription is not authenticated manuscript collation.

Keep proposed additions separate from application text. Preserve source snapshots and all historical adjudications. Append this review to the single consolidated evidence ledger without regenerating it or promoting claim decisions. The original source-fidelity denominator remains 317 blocks / 360 paragraph-and-item units; the 98-entry teaching review is a separate assessment dimension.

The review does not confer human scholarly approval or authorize a new store release. Produce a report, exact coverage and prioritized findings for a concrete, source-supported improvement batch.
