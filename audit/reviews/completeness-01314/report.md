The framing already limits this work responsibly. קדשים promises selected offerings and services, not every מנחה or the full יום הכיפורים order. טהרות excludes detailed אהל, נגעים, calendars and comprehensive ספקות, and explicitly denies presenting Rambam as unanimous. אישות excludes surveying forms of חופה and practical ceremony instructions; that still permits defining the chosen framework. זרעים excludes a practical separation formula, comprehensive geography/year rules and modern conversions. ממונות explicitly excludes full tort, יאוש, inheritance and testimony coverage. איסור והיתר is narrowed by its subtitle and limits to selected foundations. None of those declared exclusions is, by itself, a teaching defect. The current broad titles do not require a claim of full Shas coverage or a title change when read with these subtitles and limits.

The source base is already plural. Actual passages use Mishnah, Torah, Meiri, Rashi, Ramban and the two distinct Tosafot Yom Tov works alongside Rambam. Citation counts alone would not establish interpretive balance, but the text supplies consequential alternatives where selected: `z-biur/distinction` explicitly explains Ramban’s renunciation view against Rambam’s destruction view, including the retention condition.
