# Second Hebrew prose pass — 0.12.0

The complete introductory index was read again; 50 passages across all six subjects were refined. The other passages were retained where rewriting offered no clear improvement. This is a language revision, not an expansion of coverage.

Explanations now more often state the distinction directly, rather than saying that the source discusses it. Examples include receiving blood from the animal rather than collecting it from the floor, a get written before choosing which wife to divorce, and the distinction between giving first tithe to a Levite and eating second tithe in sanctity. Repetition and vague concluding sentences were reduced. The distinction between prohibiting a particular sukkah by a vow and undertaking not to perform an action was preserved in the wording.

The cited source segments for the revised passages were read during this pass: Rambam's commentary and introductions, the relevant Mishnah and Torah passages, Meiri on Ketubot and theft, and Ramban's account of shemittah biur. The dispute about biur, the attribution of the gentile-corpse rule to Rambam, practical-scope qualifications, and original textual uncertainties remain visible. No original source text, reference, attribution field or textual note changed.

There remain 89 entries, 251 blocks and 214 bundled source documents. Structural comparison confirmed that only explanation paragraphs changed within the content model. Such comparison is evidence of preservation, not proof of interpretation. This is not an independent scholarly certification or a comparison with Arabic originals.

The interface, six-subject scope, fonts, theme handling and source-opening implementation are unchanged. Native installed-app operation remains unverified. No screenshots were made.
